# E2 — Independent Sample Audit Decision

## Decision

**Branch B is adopted.** No post hoc author-run spot-check will be presented as an independent audit.

Reviewer 2 framed an independent sample audit as something that would strengthen confidence rather than as a mandatory protocol element. The review did not contain independent duplicate coding, and every author either participated in the primary review/coding or in the final analytical audit. An additional re-check performed by the same author team would therefore not constitute independent verification.

## Why a token audit is rejected

The following people are not independent auditors of this review:

|---|---|---|
| Ismail Khayoub | No | primary reviewer / corpus coding and synthesis |
| Firdaous Ait Mohamed | No | primary reviewer / corpus coding and synthesis |

A small author-run re-check would create the appearance of independent verification without meeting that standard. E2 therefore records the limitation instead of manufacturing a validation exercise after the fact.

## Manuscript limitation text — Branch B

Use in §11, coordinated with D1 rather than as a competing rewrite:

> Coding and evidence weighting were performed collaboratively by the two primary reviewers and subsequently audited within the author team. No reviewer outside the author team independently recoded a sample blind to the original labels. The thematic classification and evidence-weighting judgments therefore reflect the shared interpretation of the author team and have not been externally corroborated. To support external scrutiny, the paper-level coding, synthesis notes, source-location evidence, and audit artifacts are released in the evidence repository.

This statement must not imply that the author-team analytical audit was an independent audit.

## Reviewer-response text — Branch B

> We agree that a genuinely independent audit of a meaningful sample would strengthen confidence in the coding. We considered adding such an exercise during revision, but an audit performed by a member of the author team would not constitute independent verification because all authors either participated in the primary review/coding or in the final analytical audit. We therefore chose not to present a token author-run spot-check as independent validation. Instead, we state the absence of external independent recoding explicitly as a limitation and expose the per-record coding, synthesis notes, and supporting evidence artifacts so that readers can inspect and re-derive individual decisions.

## What would be required to switch to Branch A later

Branch A remains possible only if all of the following are satisfied before reporting it:

1. a named **non-author** auditor with relevant domain competence;
2. a codebook fixed before the audit;
3. a sample selection rule and random seed fixed before labels are inspected;
4. sampling from the final `{{N_SYNTH}}` set, preferably stratified across relevant priority/taxonomy dimensions;
5. the auditor is blind to existing labels during recoding;
6. sample-level agreement/disagreements are reported transparently;
7. any agreement statistic is described as applying to the audited sample only, not the full collaboratively coded corpus.

Until those conditions are met, the manuscript must remain on Branch B.
