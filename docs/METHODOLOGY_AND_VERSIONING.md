# Methodology and Versioning

## Corpus layers

1. **Identification ledger:** 3,462 author-approved records.
2. **Screened records after normalization/deduplication:** 3,455.
3. **Excluded during screening/eligibility:** 2,643.
4. **Provisional inclusions:** 812.
5. **Post-inclusion reconciliation:** six duplicate study representations and one non-study template artifact removed.
6. **Final systematic-mapping corpus:** 805 unique studies.
7. **Published/accepted citation-eligibility layer:** 403 studies.

## Reading depth

Ismail Khayoub and Firdaous Ait Mohamed conducted in-depth reading of P0/P1 studies and rapid targeted reading of P2/P3 studies, focusing on methods, results, limitations, and relevance. They also conducted and cross-checked screening, eligibility, coding, evidence extraction, and synthesis. Mohamed-Amine Chadi and Hajar Mousannif performed the final analytical verification and audit.

## Version policy

Canonical published metadata supersedes preprint metadata. Preprints and non-archival workshop papers remain visible in the 805-study mapping file but are excluded from the strict evidence pool unless a final published/accepted version is verified.
