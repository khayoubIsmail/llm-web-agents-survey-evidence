## 10. Thesis-ready synthesis paragraph

The S5.3 P1 literature shows that web-agent planning has evolved beyond simple reactive next-action prediction. Agent-E and Agent S2 demonstrate that hierarchical architectures can distribute planning, execution, and grounding across specialized modules. Tree Search for Language Model Agents shows that explicit test-time search improves web-agent performance, but also exposes the cost and safety limits of exploring real websites. World-model approaches such as Web Agents with World Models and WebDreamer simulate candidate outcomes before acting, reducing risky real-world exploration. WebDART is especially important for this thesis because it decomposes complex web tasks into navigation, information extraction, and execution, directly connecting planning to extraction.
