# EscapeBench: Towards Advancing Creative Intelligence of Language Model Agents

## Research problem and contribution

EscapeBench targets creative, implicit-goal reasoning through room-escape games rather than tasks with an explicit objective and standard tool use. EscapeAgent adds Foresight for unconventional affordance discovery and Reflection for tracking unresolved puzzles.

## Method and evaluation

The benchmark contains interactive escape environments with hidden objectives, long dependencies, and tools whose useful function is not stated. Baselines use chain-of-thought and working memory. Without hints, current models average only about 15% progress. EscapeAgent maintains coherent action sequences exceeding 1,000 steps and completes tasks with up to 40% fewer steps and hints, improving action success across difficulty levels.

## Limitations and critical assessment

Escape games are purpose-built to reward novelty and may not represent ordinary web work. “Creativity” is operationalized through benchmark progress and unusual tool use, not independently validated as a general capability. Despite relative gains, the authors note that difficult scenarios remain far from solved and still need hints.

## Relevance to the survey

Use for evaluation gaps around implicit goals and novel affordances. It cautions that benchmark success on known workflows does not measure adaptation when the next useful action is not obvious.
