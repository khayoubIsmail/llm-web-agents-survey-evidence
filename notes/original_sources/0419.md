### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

- **Tool space:** TheMCPCompany automatically creates MCP servers from real service REST APIs, yielding **more than 18,000 tools**.
- **Ground truth:** tasks include manually annotated task-specific tool sets, permitting an oracle comparison between perfect tool retrieval and practical retrieval.
- **Finding:** with appropriate tool retrieval, all evaluated models perform similarly to or better than browser-based agents and can reduce cost.
- **Model-scale effect:** smaller models often fail to exploit retrieved tools, while GPT-5 with retrieval approaches its ground-truth-tool performance.
- **Hard case:** even advanced reasoning models struggle when enterprise environments require discovering and composing tools from tens of thousands of candidates.
- **Status caution:** current verification found the full manuscript but not a confirmed archival published/accepted proceedings version.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The benchmark assumes service APIs can be converted into useful MCP tools and that retrieval quality can be isolated from execution quality. Publication-status eligibility must be revalidated before the paper is used as strict archival evidence.

### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Tool availability is unusually rich and may not reflect all real deployments.
- **Limitation 2:** The benchmark strongly depends on tool retrieval quality.
- **Limitation 3:** Large tool sets create retrieval and reasoning challenges that remain unsolved.

General limitation for your thesis:

```text
A benchmark evaluates only a slice of agent behavior.
No single benchmark proves that an agent can perform generalized web automation and data extraction.
```
