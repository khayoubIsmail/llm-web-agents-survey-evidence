### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

GUI-World contains 12,379 GUI videos spanning six scenarios, including software, websites, iOS, Android, multi-window settings and XR, with 10,702 training and 1,677 test samples. It generates free-form, multiple-choice and conversational questions over dynamic/sequential content and benchmarks image and video MLLMs. GPT-4o averages 84.8% MC accuracy and 3.573/5 free-form score, whereas the tested video LLMs are substantially weaker; fine-tuned GUI-Vid improves to 54.6% MC and 2.796 free-form. The paper shows that dynamic GUI understanding remains harder than static screenshot understanding.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Human keyframe annotation is labor intensive and current video LLMs remain limited by sparse GUI-video data and base-model capability. The benchmark measures understanding, not end-to-end action safety.

### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Video understanding is expensive and may not be necessary for all web tasks.
- **Limitation 2:** It evaluates understanding more than autonomous task completion.
- **Limitation 3:** Video datasets may not cover all real software/web workflows.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Better perception or representation is necessary but not sufficient.
A generalized web agent also needs planning, memory, action execution, error recovery, and evaluation.
```

Therefore, this paper should be used as **P2 support** for S5.2, not as the only foundation for the whole section.
