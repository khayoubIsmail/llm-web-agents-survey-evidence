### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

InfiGUIAgent first trains fundamental GUI understanding/grounding and then trains native hierarchical reasoning plus expectation-reflection for self-correction. The 2B model reaches 76.3% average accuracy on ScreenSpot, above ShowUI's 75.1% and the reported UGround-7B 73.3 despite its smaller size. AndroidWorld evaluation is performed on raw screenshots rather than relying on Set-of-Marks, providing a stricter test of native pixel grounding.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The authors motivate the work partly by weaknesses of text/SoM representations, but the resulting agent remains dependent on supervised synthetic reasoning data and is not a guarantee of robust recovery in arbitrary unseen applications.

### Limitations

- **Limitation 3:** EACL venue confirms academic status, but final performance should be compared with other GUI-agent systems carefully.
