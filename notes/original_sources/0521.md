---

### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Agent Workflow Memory (AWM) converts successful interaction traces into reusable workflows and retrieves those workflows when solving later tasks. The paper reports a 24.6% relative improvement on Mind2Web and a 51.1% relative improvement on WebArena; the released WebArena agent reaches 35.6% success. Online AWM also yields 8.9-14.0 absolute-point gains as the train-test distribution gap widens, indicating that reusable procedural memory can improve cross-task generalization.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Workflow quality depends on whether past trajectories encode reusable abstractions rather than accidental site-specific details. Stored workflows can also become stale when interfaces or task semantics change.
