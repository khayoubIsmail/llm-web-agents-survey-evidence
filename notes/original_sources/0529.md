#### Thesis-ready paragraph

AgentTrek contributes to S5.4 by showing that web-agent behavior can be improved through a specific training or adaptation mechanism: Synthesizes GUI/web-agent trajectories from web tutorials by converting tutorial instructions into task goals and replaying them with a VLM agent. The main result is that Tutorial-guided replay produces useful multi-step trajectories and improves GUI/web-agent grounding and planning. For the thesis, this paper is important because it moves web agents beyond prompt-only behavior and toward learned, reusable, or self-improving interaction skills.
