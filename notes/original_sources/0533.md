### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

WebEvolver couples a web-agent policy with an LLM world model that acts as a virtual server for imagined interaction. The agent and world model improve together, allowing training to scale without relying on closed-model trajectory distillation. Across Mind2Web-Live, WebVoyager and GAIA-Web, the paper reports roughly a 10% performance improvement over prior self-evolving-agent baselines, demonstrating that imagined environments can provide useful additional training experience.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

World-model errors can generate unrealistic states and reward shortcuts, so simulated improvement may not transfer to live websites. Maintaining a sufficiently faithful evolving world model also adds substantial training complexity.
