### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Web-Shepherd trains process reward models (PRMs) for step-level web-agent evaluation using WebPRM Collection, which contains about 40K preference pairs, and evaluates them on WebRewardBench. The trained verifier improves reward-model accuracy by roughly 30 points over GPT-4o in the reported comparison. When used with a GPT-4o-mini policy on WebArena-Lite, Web-Shepherd improves success by 10.9 points while costing about one tenth as much as using GPT-4o-mini itself as the verifier.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

A PRM inherits annotation and preference-construction biases, and step-level preference accuracy does not guarantee that globally optimal long-horizon trajectories receive the best cumulative score.
