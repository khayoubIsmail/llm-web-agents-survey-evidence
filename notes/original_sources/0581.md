### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

EvolveSearch alternates supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning so a search agent improves from its own progressively harder search trajectories without human-authored reasoning labels. Across seven multi-hop QA/search benchmarks, the paper reports an average improvement of about 4.7 points over the strongest prior system in its comparison. The training loop explicitly co-evolves search difficulty with agent capability rather than using a fixed query distribution.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Self-evolution can drift toward the generator's preferred query patterns, and QA-based rewards do not directly measure browser-side safety, interaction cost or source quality.

### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Retrieved experience can be stale or irrelevant.
- **Limitation 2:** Memory quality and retrieval are new bottlenecks.
- **Limitation 3:** Self-improvement loops require safeguards against error accumulation.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Training improvements in one environment do not automatically imply generalized web automation.
Agent learning still depends on perception, action grounding, evaluation validity, safety, and environment realism.
```
