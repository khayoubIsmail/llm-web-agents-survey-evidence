### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Structured Agent Distillation distinguishes reasoning tokens from action tokens and applies span-specific distillation objectives instead of treating an agent trajectory as homogeneous text. Across ALFWorld, HotPotQA-ReAct and WebShop, the structured loss consistently improves over ordinary token-level imitation/distillation and preserves much of the teacher's agent performance in a smaller student. The study isolates action/reasoning structure as useful supervision during model compression.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The method assumes trajectories can be cleanly partitioned into reasoning and action spans and inherits the teacher's policy biases. Distillation alone does not provide new exploration beyond teacher behavior.
