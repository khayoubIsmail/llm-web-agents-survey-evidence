### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Counterfactual Soft Reinforcement Learning (CoSo) estimates which language-model tokens causally influence the post-processed environment action and concentrates policy updates on those action-critical tokens. This reduces wasted exploration over reasoning tokens that do not change behavior. The paper proves policy-improvement/convergence properties under its formulation and reports consistent gains across Android control, card-game and embodied-agent tasks relative to conventional token-level online tuning.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Counterfactual influence must be approximated and can be unstable when many token sequences map to the same action. The method also assumes a differentiable/tractable relation between generated text and the environment action parser.
