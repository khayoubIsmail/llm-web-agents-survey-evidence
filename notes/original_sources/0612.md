### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Agent Data Protocol (ADP) defines a common interlingua for observations, reasoning, tool calls and environment feedback so heterogeneous agent datasets can be jointly fine-tuned instead of requiring environment-specific formatting. The paper converts 13 datasets spanning coding, browsing, tool use and deep research. Joint SFT improves average performance by roughly 20% over the base model and reaches state-of-the-art or near-state-of-the-art results across several domains without domain-specific tuning.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

A common schema can erase environment-specific semantics, and dataset mixture weights strongly affect transfer. Format unification also does not by itself resolve conflicting supervision among source datasets.

### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Synthetic trajectories can contain artifacts.
- **Limitation 2:** Data quality matters more than raw volume.
- **Limitation 3:** Training data may not generalize to changing websites or new apps.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Training improvements in one environment do not automatically imply generalized web automation.
Agent learning still depends on perception, action grounding, evaluation validity, safety, and environment realism.
```

Therefore, use this paper as **P2 support** unless it becomes central to the S5.4 subsection.
