### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

ColorBrowserAgent adapts without model retraining by converting sparse expert feedback after failures into a persistent site-specific knowledge base and retrieving that knowledge during future execution. A progressive summarizer keeps a compressed belief state aligned with retrieved rules to reduce long-horizon decision drift. The paper reports 71.2% WebArena success, 47.4% zero-shot transfer on WebChoreArena, and a 19.3% relative increase in user satisfaction in commercial deployment.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Cold-start domains still require human interventions to populate useful knowledge, and external memories can become stale as websites change. Progressive summaries can also omit evidence needed later in a long task.
