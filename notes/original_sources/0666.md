### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

The final systematic review covers 301 primary web-scraping studies published from 2004-2025, including 198 journal papers and 103 conference studies. It maps classic and modern extraction techniques, programming languages/tools, evaluation metrics, application areas and dataset sizes, and explicitly adds LLM-enhanced scraping plus legal/ethical guidance. The full preprint also treats web scraping as a corpus-collection methodology rather than only a technical extraction routine. For this survey it supplies broad contextual evidence on the extraction literature but not a web-agent benchmark itself.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

As a broad review, it aggregates heterogeneous scraping tasks and metrics rather than providing one controlled agent evaluation. The preprint and final journal version have different corpus counts (260 versus 301), so claims must use the final published 301-study count.
