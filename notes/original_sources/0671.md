### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

This paper surveys practical web-data-collection challenges and the Python tooling used to address them, covering HTTP/HTML retrieval, parsing, dynamic content, access restrictions, storage/cleaning and legal/ethical concerns. Its value for the survey is methodological context: a production extraction pipeline requires more than a selector—it must handle request behavior, page rendering, data cleaning and responsible collection. The paper does not present an LLM-agent architecture or competitive extraction benchmark and should therefore be cited only as supporting scraping-practice context.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The work is descriptive rather than a controlled benchmark, and its Python-centered discussion does not demonstrate generalization across arbitrary websites or LLM-based adaptive extraction.
