### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

ProductAgent treats extraction/search as an interactive clarification problem: it maintains structured dialogue memory, summarizes product features, generates clarification questions and repeatedly retrieves from hybrid symbolic+dense indices. PROCLARE contains a one-million-product document collection across 20 categories and 2,000 simulated conversational sessions, producing 10,000 turn-specific queries. Retrieval performance improves monotonically as dialogue turns make the desired product constraints more explicit, empirically validating clarification as a mechanism for refining an underspecified extraction/retrieval target.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The benchmark relies on an LLM user simulator rather than real users and focuses on product retrieval, not arbitrary schema-guided record extraction. Simulator behavior can make clarification easier or more regular than natural conversations.
