### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

AdvAgent trains a black-box adversarial prompter from feedback produced by a victim web agent rather than requiring gradients or model internals. Against the GPT-4V-powered SeeAct agent, the paper reports standard-setting attack success rates around the mid/high-90s across finance, medical, housing and cooking tasks (the SFT+DPO variants reach roughly 94%-100% depending on domain). Transfer tests remain strong under changed injection positions and modified HTML fields, while prompt-based defenses only partially reduce vulnerability.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The framework assumes the attacker can inject content into a webpage encountered by the agent, and optimization requires repeated black-box interactions with the victim. Results are centered on SeeAct/GPT-4V and may vary with newer agent architectures and permission controls.
