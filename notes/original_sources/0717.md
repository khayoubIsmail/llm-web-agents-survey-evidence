### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

TrickyArena embeds controllable deceptive UI patterns into realistic e-commerce, streaming and news-style web applications, while LiteAgent records fine-grained agent interactions. Six web-agent systems built on Claude 3.7 Sonnet, GPT-4o and Gemini 2.5 Pro are evaluated. With one dark pattern present, agents are susceptible on average 41% of the time; some higher-performing agents exceed roughly 69%-72% susceptibility. Combining multiple patterns can turn individually ineffective deceptions into successful manipulations, and both visual styling and HTML implementation affect vulnerability.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The study uses controlled replicas and a selected taxonomy of dark patterns. Susceptibility therefore depends on benchmark design and does not directly quantify harm after a deceptive action.
