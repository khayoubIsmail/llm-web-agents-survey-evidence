### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

AprielGuard is an 8B safeguard model trained to classify both ordinary safety risks and adversarial manipulation within one framework, including standalone prompts, multi-turn conversations and agentic workflows. Its disclosed training mixture contains 327,714 examples across safe/unsafe, adversarial/non-adversarial and conversational/standalone cells, with context lengths up to 32k. Public evaluation spans content-safety and adversarial datasets, while the internal agentic workflow evaluation injects attacks into prompts, reasoning traces, tool parameters, memory and inter-agent communication. In the released agentic evaluation, the non-reasoning configuration reports safety F1 0.97 and adversarial F1 0.88; reasoning mode reports safety F1 0.95 and adversarial F1 0.94. The model therefore targets guardrail coverage across both conversational and tool-using agent contexts rather than treating prompt injection as a separate detector.

#### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Several agentic and long-context evaluations are proprietary/internal, reducing independent reproducibility of those specific scores. Public benchmark performance does not establish robustness to unseen adaptive attacks, and later third-party testing can expose bypasses not represented in the authors' benchmark suite.
