## Core synthesis paragraph

```text
Reliability research for LLM-based agents increasingly treats failure as a structured, diagnosable phenomenon rather than a binary outcome. 
Automatic evaluators can judge trajectories and guide refinement; failure taxonomies expose recurring errors in planning, action, memory, reflection, environment interaction, and multi-agent coordination; OOD and localization diagnostics reveal perception-level risk; and safety benchmarks show that agents may blindly pursue goals even when tasks are unsafe, ambiguous, or infeasible. 
For web automation and data extraction, these findings imply that agent architectures must include explicit verification, uncertainty detection, failure recovery, and safety controls.
```
