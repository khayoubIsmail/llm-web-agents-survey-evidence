#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

- **Zero-shot setting:** the agent is evaluated without expert demonstrations or environment-specific training traces.
- **Method:** it generates executable computer actions while maintaining structured thoughts and reflecting on execution feedback.
- **Result pattern:** the system exceeds recent baselines on many easier MiniWoB++ tasks and is competitive with strong specialized methods on harder tasks despite not using demonstrations or extra screen annotations.
- **Efficiency:** structured reflection is used to reduce repeated mistakes and unnecessary action loops rather than only increasing raw action count.
- **Survey role:** the paper provides early evidence that explicit self-reflection can improve browser control without task-specific finetuning.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

MiniWoB++ is far simpler than the live Web, and success on synthetic widgets does not establish cross-site generalization. Reflection can also amplify an incorrect interpretation of environment state.

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Preprint status means it should be cited carefully.
- **Limitation 2:** Zero-shot reflection can still be wrong or ungrounded.
- **Limitation 3:** It may not scale reliably to complex real-world websites and desktop workflows.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Improving one agent component is not enough for generalized web automation.
A robust web agent still needs grounding, planning, execution, verification, recovery, and safety.
```
