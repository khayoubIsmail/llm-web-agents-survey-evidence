#### Key finding / main claim

Foundation models can reduce manual development effort, but enterprise automation demands reliability, governance, and integration with existing systems.

For S4, the important question is not only whether the method works on its benchmark, but **which part of web/GUI agency it improves**:

- perception and grounding,
- planning and reasoning,
- action execution,
- API/tool use,
- memory/state tracking,
- recovery and verification,
- or deployment/evaluation.

---

#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

- **Benchmark:** ECLAIR evaluates **30 enterprise workflows** collected from realistic business-software tasks.
- **Workflow understanding:** the strongest configuration reaches about **93% SOP/action understanding accuracy** (versus roughly 60% for the weakest tested setup).
- **End-to-end gap:** actual autonomous workflow completion is only about **40%**, far below the workflow-understanding score.
- **Enterprise motivation:** traditional RPA deployments can require **12–18 months** of setup and initially operate near **60% accuracy** in the cited case studies.
- **Survey implication:** converting a demonstration into a high-quality procedure is easier than robustly executing that procedure in changing enterprise GUIs.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Thirty workflows cover only a small fraction of enterprise applications and policies. High SOP accuracy does not remove GUI grounding, authentication, side-effect or recovery failures during execution.

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Preprint status.
