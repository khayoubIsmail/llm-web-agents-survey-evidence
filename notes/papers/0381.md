#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

- **Coverage:** VisualAgentBench spans **5 environments** across embodied, GUI and visual-design tasks with **746 test instances**.
- **Training resource:** it releases **4,482 trajectories** (about 11.2 turns on average) for behavior-cloning development, not only evaluation.
- **Models:** the paper evaluates **9 proprietary and 8 open LMMs**.
- **Result:** GPT-4o is strongest overall at roughly **36.2% average success**; the best finetuned open model, InternVL2, is about **16.0%**.
- **Uneven capability:** GPT-4o varies sharply by environment (for example about 55.2% Minecraft versus 18.2% CSS in the reported table), demonstrating that an aggregate agent score hides domain-specific weaknesses.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

High-level action interfaces simplify low-level motor control, and the five environments still represent a bounded task sample. Proprietary models are prompted while open models are finetuned, so model-family comparisons also reflect different adaptation regimes.
