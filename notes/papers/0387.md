#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

- **Benchmark:** VideoWebArena contains **2,021 Web-agent tasks** grounded in manually curated video tutorials totaling almost **4 hours**.
- **Task types:** skill retention asks agents to execute a demonstrated workflow; factual retention requires extracting video information to complete a Web task.
- **Result:** the best factual-retention task success is only **13.3%**, while intermediate factual QA reaches **45.8%**, versus humans at **73.9% task success / 79.3% QA**.
- **Counterintuitive finding:** supplying tutorials in context can hurt skill execution—about **5% lower on WebArena and 10.3% lower on VisualWebArena**—showing that long multimodal context may become distracting noise.
- **Implication:** retrieving the right video fact and using it in a browser trajectory are separate capabilities.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Video processing is expensive and model-context strategies strongly affect results. The benchmark's tutorial/task distribution cannot represent all real-world video-to-Web workflows.

---

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Web environments are dynamic and hard to reproduce.
- **Limitation 2:** LLM-based or trajectory-based evaluation can be noisy.
- **Limitation 3:** Strong results may depend on specific scaffolds, browsers, websites, or task selection.
