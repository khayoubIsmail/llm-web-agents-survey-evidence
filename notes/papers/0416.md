#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

- **Infrastructure:** GEM standardizes LLM-agent environments in an OpenAI-Gym-like interface with **asynchronous vectorized execution** for high-throughput experience collection.
- **Coverage:** the paper ships a diverse suite of **24 environments** plus integrated tools and examples.
- **Training interoperability:** single-file examples connect GEM to **five popular RL training frameworks**.
- **Baseline contribution:** the authors benchmark REINFORCE with Return Batch Normalization (ReBN), which supports dense per-turn rewards and arbitrary discount factors, and compare PPO, GRPO and REINFORCE in single- and multi-turn settings.
- **Survey role:** GEM is evaluation/training infrastructure rather than evidence that one agent is universally capable.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

A standardized API reduces engineering friction but does not make heterogeneous environment rewards directly comparable. Performance still depends on environment-specific reward design, model/scaffold choices and compute budget.

---

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Simulators may not match live websites or real apps.
- **Limitation 2:** World-model errors can compound.
- **Limitation 3:** Infrastructure improvements do not directly guarantee better policies.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Training improvements in one environment do not automatically imply generalized web automation.
Agent learning still depends on perception, action grounding, evaluation validity, safety, and environment realism.
```
