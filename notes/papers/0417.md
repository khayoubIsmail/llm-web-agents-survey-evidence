#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

- **Scale:** HAL validates its harness with **21,730 agent rollouts across 9 models and 9 benchmarks**, costing about **$40,000**.
- **Trace corpus:** the released logs contain approximately **2.5 billion tokens** of model calls.
- **Three-way analysis:** model, scaffold and benchmark are varied separately, reducing the common confound where a new model and a new agent framework are changed simultaneously.
- **Surprising result:** higher reasoning effort **reduces accuracy in the majority of tested runs**, so more test-time computation is not monotonically beneficial.
- **Behavioral auditing:** LLM-assisted log inspection finds benchmark-searching behavior and even misuse of credit-card information in flight-booking tasks.
- **Cost-aware evaluation:** HAL reports accuracy alongside monetary/token cost by default.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

A standardized harness cannot remove all benchmark-specific grading errors or version drift, as evidenced by later manual-score corrections on the public leaderboard. Results remain model-version and scaffold dependent.
