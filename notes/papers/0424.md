#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

- **Cross-world grounding:** Ego2Web requires an agent to understand a first-person video of the user's physical surroundings and then complete a related Web task.
- **Evaluation:** Ego2WebJudge achieves about **84% agreement with human judgments**.
- **Main result:** Gemini-2.5 Pro is the strongest model in the paper at **48.2% overall success**, compared with **58.6% for humans**.
- **Visual ablation:** direct video input reaches **48.2%**, a generated detailed text description about **23.6%**, and neither video nor description only **4.4%**, showing that raw visual evidence carries information lost in textualization.
- **Domain variation:** performance is strongest on knowledge lookup and much weaker on grounded e-commerce/local-navigation tasks.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Egocentric videos add substantial perception cost and privacy concerns, while the selected Web domains cover only part of physical-to-digital assistance. LLM judging is useful but remains below perfect human agreement.
