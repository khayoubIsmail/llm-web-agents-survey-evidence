---

#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

ScreenAI is a roughly 5B-parameter UI/infographics vision-language model that combines PaLI-style modeling with Pix2Struct-style flexible patching. A screen-annotation task teaches element type/location/description, and those annotations are used to synthesize QA, navigation and summarization data. The model reports new state-of-the-art results on Multi-page DocVQA, WebSRC, MoTIF and Widget Captioning and best-in-class performance among similar-size models on ChartQA, DocVQA and InfographicVQA. Scaling experiments show continued gains through the largest tested model.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

ScreenAI is an understanding model and does not itself execute computer actions. Performance also depends on automatically generated training data and remains bounded by visual resolution and annotation quality.

---

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Screen understanding does not equal autonomous GUI interaction.
- **Limitation 2:** It may need additional grounding/action modules to become an agent.
- **Limitation 3:** Screen-specific datasets may not cover dynamic interaction states.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Better perception or representation is necessary but not sufficient.
A generalized web agent also needs planning, memory, action execution, error recovery, and evaluation.
```

Therefore, this paper should be used as **P2 support** for S5.2, not as the only foundation for the whole section.
