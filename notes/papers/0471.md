#### Key finding / main claim

Existing GUI grounding models perform poorly; the best model reaches only 18.9%, while ScreenSeekeR improves to 48.1% by narrowing the search area.

For S5.2, the important point is not only the final benchmark score.

---

#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

ScreenSpot-Pro targets high-resolution professional desktop interfaces rather than consumer/mobile screens. The paper release contains 1,581 expert-annotated instruction-image pairs across 23 professional applications, five industries and three operating systems; target elements occupy only about 0.07% of the screen area on average. Prior GUI grounding models perform poorly—the paper reports a best existing score around 18.9%—while the proposed ScreenSeekeR reaches 48.1 without additional grounding training, exposing a large professional-GUI generalization gap.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The benchmark intentionally isolates grounding and does not evaluate end-to-end planning/execution, partly because of software licensing and safety constraints. High-resolution professional applications therefore remain under-tested at the full-agent level.
