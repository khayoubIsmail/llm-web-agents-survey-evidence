#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

The paper distinguishes scaling internal reasoning from scaling actual environment interaction. Test-Time Interaction (TTI) increases the rollout horizon so an agent can gather information, explore, backtrack and re-plan; the training version uses curriculum-based online RL that adapts allowed interaction length. With Gemma-3-12B, TTI is reported as a state-of-the-art open-source/open-data agent on WebVoyager and WebArena, and analysis shows longer interaction budgets improve the exploration/exploitation balance even without proportionally increasing per-step reasoning.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

More interactions increase latency, cost and exposure to harmful/irreversible actions. Longer horizons also amplify compounding action and observation errors when recovery is weak.

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** The method may be benchmark-specific.
- **Limitation 2:** Planning improvements can increase compute cost.
- **Limitation 3:** Success in controlled environments may not transfer fully to live web automation.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Better planning does not automatically mean generalized web automation is solved.
The agent still needs perception, grounding, execution, verification, recovery, and safety.
```
