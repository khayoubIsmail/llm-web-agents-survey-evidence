#### Simple understanding

MANGO improves web navigation by giving agents a global view of the website before navigation. Instead of always starting from the homepage, it crawls/searches for candidate URLs and uses Thompson Sampling to allocate navigation attempts.

```text

Paper → This work adds planning, search, memory, reflection, world modeling, skill reuse, or context management.

```

#### Key finding / main claim

MANGO reports higher success on WebVoyager and WebWalkerQA, especially for complex websites where starting from the root URL wastes budget.

---

#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Mango avoids always starting from a site's root by building a global view of candidate URLs, initializing relevance from lightweight crawling/search, and using Thompson Sampling to allocate navigation budget across entry points. An episodic memory prevents repeated unsuccessful exploration. With GPT-5-mini it reports 63.6% success on WebVoyager, 7.3 points above the strongest comparison, and 52.5% on WebWalkerQA, 26.8 points above the best baseline. The results quantify the value of choosing where to begin exploration, not only what action to take next.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The method assumes a crawlable/discoverable URL structure and can be less applicable to heavily client-rendered, authenticated or state-dependent sites. Global crawling also adds up-front cost.
