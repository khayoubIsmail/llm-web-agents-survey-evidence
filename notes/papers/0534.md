---

#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Explorer scales real-web trajectory collection through automated exploration rather than tutorial replay alone. The dataset contains more than 94K successful trajectories spanning about 49K URLs, 720K screenshots and 33M webpage elements; the paper reports an average cost near $0.28 per successful trajectory. Data-scaling experiments show consistent gains on Mind2Web-Live, Multimodal-Mind2Web and MiniWoB++, linking broader exploration coverage to better agent generalization.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Successful-trajectory filtering can bias the dataset toward easier or more discoverable tasks, while broad exploration still incurs live-site cost and may miss rare but important failure states.
