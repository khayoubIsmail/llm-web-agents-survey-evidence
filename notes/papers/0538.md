#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Go-Browse structures web exploration as a graph so data collection can systematically expand novel states instead of repeatedly following similar trajectories. Its training set contains about 10K successful trajectories and 40K interaction steps across roughly 100 websites. The resulting 7B agent reaches 21.7% WebArena success, 2.4 points above GPT-4o-mini and 2.9 points above the strongest sub-10B comparison reported in the paper.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Graph exploration can still be biased by the starting sites and exploration policy, and successful coverage of one website graph does not guarantee transfer after major interface changes.
