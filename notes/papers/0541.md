#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

WebAgent-R1 trains directly from multi-turn online web interaction with a sparse binary task-completion reward and asynchronous trajectory collection. Qwen2.5-3B improves from 6.1% to 33.9% and Llama-3.1-8B from 8.5% to 44.8% in the paper's principal web-agent evaluation, surpassing substantially larger prompting and supervised baselines. The result demonstrates that end-to-end multi-turn RL can produce large gains even without dense step rewards.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Sparse binary rewards make credit assignment difficult and require many expensive online rollouts. Training can also over-specialize to benchmark/task generators if environment diversity is insufficient.
