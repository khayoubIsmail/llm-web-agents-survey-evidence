#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

TongUI converts internet-scale multimodal tutorials into GUI trajectories and releases GUI-Net-1M, containing about one million trajectories across five operating systems and more than 280 applications. Fine-tuning Qwen2.5-VL models at 3B, 7B and 32B scale yields more than 10% improvement over comparison baselines on multiple GUI grounding and navigation benchmarks, showing that tutorial-derived supervision scales beyond a single platform.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Tutorial-derived data inherits documentation bias and may omit undocumented or adversarial interaction patterns. Cross-platform action normalization can also hide platform-specific semantics.
