#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

This study systematically evaluates 1,370 web-agent training configurations rather than proposing a single recipe. A Llama-3.1-8B student first imitates a Llama-3.3-70B teacher and is then improved with on-policy RL. SFT+RL consistently outperforms either method alone on WorkArena and MiniWoB++; on MiniWoB++ the hybrid recipe reaches the peak pure-SFT performance using about 55% of its compute. The statistical analysis identifies data quality, curriculum and on-policy refinement as stronger predictors than simply scaling imitation data.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The diagnosis is tied to the tested model family and benchmark suite; interactions between hyperparameters may differ for other environments. Statistical association across configurations also does not prove every factor is causally independent.
