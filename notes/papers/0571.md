#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

AgentRM studies reward models that generalize across agent tasks rather than being trained for one environment. Its 8B reward model is evaluated over nine tasks in four categories and is used for Best-of-N and beam-search policy improvement. The paper reports an average 8.8-point gain for non-finetuned 8B agents, about four points over the strongest general reward-model comparison, and a 12.6-point weak-to-strong improvement when guiding Llama-3-70B. Specialized task reward models improve by about 11.4 points on average.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Reward-model generalization is still imperfect across qualitatively different environments, and search gains depend on candidate diversity. A reward model that systematically mis-scores a failure mode can amplify that error through Best-of-N selection.

---

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Reward models can be miscalibrated or exploited.
- **Limitation 2:** Step-level labels may be ambiguous in GUI/web tasks.
- **Limitation 3:** Better rewards do not remove the need for safe exploration and robust evaluation.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Training improvements in one environment do not automatically imply generalized web automation.
Agent learning still depends on perception, action grounding, evaluation validity, safety, and environment realism.
```

Therefore, use this paper as **P2 support** unless it becomes central to the S5.4 subsection.
