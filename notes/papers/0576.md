#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

DeepResearcher trains a deep-research agent end to end in real web environments rather than optimizing only retrieval or answer generation. Its multi-agent browsing setup exposes the policy to live search, reading, evidence aggregation and final synthesis under reinforcement learning. The paper reports gains of up to 28.9 percentage points over prompting baselines and up to 7.2 points over RAG-oriented RL approaches, with qualitative evidence of emergent planning, cross-source validation, self-reflection and explicit uncertainty handling.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Real-web RL is expensive and nonstationary, and reward design for long-form research can conflate answer quality with citation/evidence quality. Search-engine and site changes also reduce reproducibility.
