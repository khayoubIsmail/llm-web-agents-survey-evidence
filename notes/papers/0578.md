#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

This work uses a mid-training stage on data-rich reasoning tasks before GUI-agent post-training, testing eleven auxiliary tasks to determine which capabilities transfer to computer use. Multimodal mathematical reasoning alone improves AndroidWorld by 6.3 points; text-only math improves WebArena by 5.6 and AndroidWorld by 5.4; the mixed curriculum yields gains of 8.0 on WebArena and 12.2 on AndroidWorld. GUI-perception-only mid-training gives much smaller gains, suggesting that transferable reasoning data can be more valuable than narrow GUI imitation.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Transfer depends strongly on the auxiliary-task mixture and can introduce negative transfer. Improvements do not imply that mid-training covers environment-specific grounding, long-horizon recovery or safety.

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Synthetic trajectories can contain artifacts.
- **Limitation 2:** Data quality matters more than raw volume.
- **Limitation 3:** Training data may not generalize to changing websites or new apps.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Training improvements in one environment do not automatically imply generalized web automation.
Agent learning still depends on perception, action grounding, evaluation validity, safety, and environment realism.
```
