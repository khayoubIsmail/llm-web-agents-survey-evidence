#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

PC Agent-E studies data-efficient computer-use training from only 312 human-annotated trajectories supplemented with synthetic action decisions generated by Claude-3.7. Despite the small human set, the trained agent achieves a 141% relative improvement over its starting point and surpasses Claude-3.7 extended-thinking in the paper's WindowsAgentArena-V2 evaluation, while also transferring to OSWorld. The result shows that carefully structured decision supervision can be more efficient than collecting large raw trajectory corpora.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The synthetic supervision depends on a strong proprietary teacher and may transfer its systematic mistakes. A few hundred demonstrations also cannot cover the long tail of desktop applications and safety-sensitive workflows.
