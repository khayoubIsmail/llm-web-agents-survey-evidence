#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Group-in-Group Policy Optimization (GiGPO) improves multi-turn credit assignment by combining a macro group of complete trajectories with micro groups of alternative actions from shared anchor states. The micro groups identify which local decisions caused divergence without requiring a learned critic. Relative to GRPO, the paper reports gains above 12% on ALFWorld and above 9% on WebShop while using the same rollout/GPU-memory regime and little additional wall-clock cost.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Useful micro groups require repeated or deliberately sampled anchor states; sparse state overlap reduces the benefit. Relative advantages can also be noisy when downstream outcomes are highly stochastic.
