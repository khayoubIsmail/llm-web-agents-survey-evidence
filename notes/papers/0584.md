#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

MobileIPL improves mobile-agent reasoning by constructing a tree of candidate action-thought trajectories, scoring terminal branches with executable rules, back-propagating outcome feedback, and converting good/bad branches into trajectory-level DPO preferences. It also evolves task instructions in three stages using screenshot-grounded GPT-4o generation. The resulting model is reported state of the art on three mobile GUI benchmarks and improves out-of-distribution performance relative to OS-ATLAS/UI-TARS comparisons.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Preference quality depends on rule-based terminal scoring and tree coverage. The task-evolution generator may also introduce unrealistic instructions or overrepresent visually salient workflows.
