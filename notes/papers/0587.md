#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

This work lets agents retain their own successful trajectories as in-context demonstrations and selects examples according to estimated utility rather than relying on a fixed human exemplar set. Naively adding successful self-generated examples raises ALFWorld from 73% to 89%, Wordcraft from 55% to 64%, and InterCode-SQL from 75% to 79%; the full curated method reaches 93% on ALFWorld in the final NeurIPS version. The result demonstrates a lightweight self-improvement route that requires no parameter update.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Self-generated memory can become biased toward tasks the agent already solves, leaving hard failure modes underrepresented. Incorrectly judged successes can also poison the demonstration database.

---

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Retrieved experience can be stale or irrelevant.

- **Limitation 3:** Self-improvement loops require safeguards against error accumulation.
