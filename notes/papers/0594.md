#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

WebDancer uses a four-stage training pipeline: synthetic data generation, web-trajectory sampling, supervised cold start and reinforcement learning for autonomous information seeking. For the QwQ-32B backbone, the paper reports GAIA improving from 37.8% for vanilla ReAct to 51.5% and WebWalkerQA from 24.1% to 47.9%. For Qwen-7B, GAIA improves from 18.4% to 31.0% and WebWalkerQA from 24.2% to 36.0%, showing that the training recipe transfers across model scales.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The pipeline is optimized for information seeking rather than arbitrary transactional web control, and final quality remains sensitive to synthetic-query difficulty, search-engine availability and reward correctness.
