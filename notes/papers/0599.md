#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

AgentCPM-GUI is an 8B mobile-use model trained through large grounding pretraining, supervised agent training and GRPO-based reinforcement fine-tuning. The paper reports about 12M grounding pretraining examples, 55K Chinese interaction trajectories containing roughly 470K steps, and 6.9M SFT instances. On the CAGUI benchmark it reaches 96.9 task-matching and 91.3 exact-matching scores, while its compact action format averages only about 9.7 generated tokens per action.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The very large pretraining mixture makes it difficult to isolate which data source drives transfer, and mobile/Chinese-heavy data may not generalize uniformly to desktop/web applications or other languages.
