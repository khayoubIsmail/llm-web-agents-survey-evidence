#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Contextual Experience Replay (CER) is a training-free self-improvement method that distills interaction experience into reusable environment dynamics and skills, then retrieves the relevant distilled context on later tasks. On WebArena, CER-hybrid reaches 36.7% average success versus 24.3% for the BrowserGym baseline, about a 51% relative improvement. On VisualWebArena it reaches 31.9% versus 26.2%, about a 21.8% relative gain.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Distilled experience can become stale as interfaces change and retrieval may inject irrelevant rules. Since the model parameters do not change, performance is bounded by the base agent's ability to follow retrieved experience.

---

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Retrieved experience can be stale or irrelevant.
- **Limitation 2:** Memory quality and retrieval are new bottlenecks.
- **Limitation 3:** Self-improvement loops require safeguards against error accumulation.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Training improvements in one environment do not automatically imply generalized web automation.
Agent learning still depends on perception, action grounding, evaluation validity, safety, and environment realism.
```
