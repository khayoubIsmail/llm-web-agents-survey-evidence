#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

AgentPRM decomposes step quality into promise—the expected future probability of success—and progress—the advantage gained by the current action. It learns these signals with temporal-difference and generalized-advantage targets rather than requiring dense human step labels. The paper reports roughly 8x better compute efficiency than process-value/ordinary outcome-reward alternatives; in an 8x8 WebShop search setting, a Qwen2.5-3B policy guided by AgentPRM exceeds the process-value-model comparison by more than 20 success points.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Promise estimates can be poorly calibrated in novel states, while progress depends on a meaningful local baseline. Reward decomposition does not remove search cost or guarantee safety under distribution shift.
