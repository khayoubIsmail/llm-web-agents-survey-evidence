#### Simple understanding

ColorBrowserAgent targets production web automation where agents face heterogeneous sites and long-horizon decision drift. It avoids frequent retraining by externalizing site-specific expert knowledge into a persistent knowledge base and stabilizing long trajectories with progressive summarization.
