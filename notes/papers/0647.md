#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

GEM detects GUI instructions that are outside an agent's learned capability by modeling distances in its semantic embedding space with a Gaussian mixture. Across eight smartphone, computer and browser datasets, it improves OOD-detection accuracy by an average 23.70% over the strongest baseline, while adding 4.9% training time and 6.5% test time. Across nine backbones the pattern generalizes, and escalating detected OOD cases to a cloud model improves step-wise task success by 9.40%.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Capability boundaries inferred from embedding clusters can shift after model updates or interface changes. Escalation also assumes access to a stronger external model and does not prevent failures on in-distribution instructions that the base agent still executes incorrectly.

---

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** OOD boundaries can shift as apps and environments evolve.
- **Limitation 2:** The method detects capability mismatch but does not itself solve the task.
- **Limitation 3:** Requires representative in-distribution training data.
