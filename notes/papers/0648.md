#### Key finding / main claim

GUI agent localization errors are not random; many incorrect coordinates lie close to the target or are biased by surrounding elements. Context-aware cropping can improve robustness without training.

#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

The paper shows that a single point-accuracy score hides qualitatively different GUI grounding failures. Predictions are separated into four localization categories, and Peak Sharpness Score (PSS) measures whether coordinate-token logits form a semantically coherent peak rather than an arbitrary high-confidence point. The analysis exposes systematic spatial/localization biases across GUI models. Context-Aware Cropping then uses the uncertainty/bias diagnosis to adaptively narrow visual context and consistently improves grounding robustness without retraining.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

PSS relies on access to coordinate-token logits and therefore is not available for many closed models. Cropping can also remove context needed to disambiguate visually similar targets, and better localization does not by itself solve task planning.

---

#### Limitations

- **Limitation 1:** Focuses on localization/grounding, not full long-horizon task success.
- **Limitation 2:** Evaluation mainly targets coordinate prediction behavior.
- **Limitation 3:** Training-free cropping helps but does not remove deeper planning or perception failures.

General thesis-level limitation:

```text
Failure analysis is necessary but not sufficient.
A taxonomy or benchmark identifies the problem, but reliable deployment also needs mitigation, verification, monitoring, and safety constraints.
```
