#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

The paper adds adversarial pop-ups to OSWorld and VisualWebArena and measures whether vision-language computer agents abandon the intended task to click the distraction. Across tested agents, average popup attack success is 86% and normal task success decreases by 47%. Simple defenses such as instructing the agent to ignore pop-ups or adding an advertisement notice do not reliably prevent the behavior, showing that visual distraction is a distinct risk from textual jailbreaks.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The attack family is deliberately popup-centric and does not cover all visually embedded threats. Susceptibility can depend on agent grounding and viewport configuration, and stronger UI provenance or browser-level filtering could change results.
