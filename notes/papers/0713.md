---

#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

VisualTrap poisons visual-grounding pretraining so an otherwise correct textual action plan is redirected to an attacker-controlled trigger location. On Qwen2-VL variants, full/vision poisoning produces average grounding attack-success rates around 94%-96% while maintaining clean-input grounding accuracy near the unpoisoned model. Even with only 5% poisoned data, Qwen2.5-VL-3B reaches about 90% ASR; a weaker LLaVA-NeXT-Mistral setup still reaches roughly 60%. Triggers learned on mobile/web also transfer to desktop and survive later clean fine-tuning.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The attacker requires influence over grounding pretraining data, a stronger supply-chain capability than ordinary webpage injection. Effectiveness also depends on the target model's visual grounding competence and trigger rendering.
