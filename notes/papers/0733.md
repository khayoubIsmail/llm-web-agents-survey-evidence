#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Agent Smith demonstrates an 'infectious jailbreak' in which one adversarial image placed into one multimodal agent's memory can spread harmful behavior through ordinary agent-to-agent communication. The paper simulates populations up to one million LLaVA-1.5 agents and shows exponential propagation under randomized pair-wise interaction without further attacker intervention. It also derives a condition that a defense must satisfy to provably prevent continued spread, showing that multi-agent safety cannot be assessed only at the individual-agent boundary.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The million-agent experiment is simulated with a specific interaction process and model family rather than a deployed multi-agent service. Real systems may have different communication graphs, memory isolation and content filters.
