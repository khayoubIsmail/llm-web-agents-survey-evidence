#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

HAICOSYSTEM simulates multi-turn human-agent interactions with tool use across healthcare, finance, education and four other domains. It runs 1,840 simulations from 92 scenarios and evaluates operational, content, societal and legal risks. Both proprietary and open models exhibit safety risks in more than 50% of cases, with risk generally increasing under simulated malicious users. The framework therefore broadens agent safety beyond harmful output to interactional and tool-mediated consequences.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

Users, tools and environments are simulated, and the quality of the safety conclusions depends on simulation realism and evaluator definitions. The scenario set is diverse but not exhaustive.
