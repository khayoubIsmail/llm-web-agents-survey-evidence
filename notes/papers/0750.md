#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

MobileSafetyBench evaluates safety separately from helpfulness in realistic Android-emulator interactions involving messaging, web navigation, social media, calendar and financial applications. It contains 250 tasks in total, including 200 daily-scenario tasks plus indirect-prompt-injection evaluations. Baseline frontier agents often complete requested actions while failing to avoid harmful side effects; explicit safety-prioritization prompting improves behavior but leaves a substantial safety gap. This provides direct evidence that successful device control and safe device control are distinct objectives.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The environment uses emulators and a fixed app/task set, and mobile safety judgments depend on device state and scenario assumptions. Prompting mitigation is not a hard runtime guarantee.
