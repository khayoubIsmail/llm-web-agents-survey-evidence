#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

Active Environment Injection Attack (AEIA) models malicious content as part of the mobile environment rather than a direct user prompt. AEIA-MN exploits both adversarial content injection and reasoning-gap vulnerabilities during Android agent execution. Across the evaluated MLLM agents, combining the two vulnerabilities yields attack success as high as 93% on AndroidWorld. The paper also considers attackers with only partial or zero observability of device state, showing that precise internal-agent access is not required.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The concrete attack mechanism is Android/mobile-specific and centers on environmental notification-style channels. Results may differ for desktop/web agents with different event filtering and UI provenance controls.
