#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

AgentGhost exploits mobile GUI agents' interaction history, environment state and task progress as hidden backdoor-trigger dimensions. Composite triggers are trained with supervised contrastive and supervised fine-tuning objectives so the malicious behavior activates conditionally while normal utility is preserved. Across two mobile benchmarks and three attack objectives, attack accuracy reaches 99.7% with only about 1% clean-utility degradation. The proposed defense reduces attack accuracy to 22.1%, demonstrating both the severity of supply-chain backdoors and the possibility of targeted mitigation.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

The threat model requires compromise of the model/fine-tuning supply chain. The tailored defense is evaluated against AgentGhost-style triggers and may not generalize to unrelated backdoor mechanisms.
