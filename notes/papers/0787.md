#### Concrete evidence verified from the full paper

MCP Security Bench (MSB) evaluates attacks across all three MCP tool-use stages: task planning, tool invocation and response handling. The final benchmark contains 12 attack types and 2,000 test cases across 10 domains, 65 realistic tasks and 405 benign/malicious tools, executed through MCP rather than simulated text-only tool outputs. Experiments on 10 LLM agents show that MCP-specific attacks are readily exploitable; the paper reports peak ASR of 75.83% and observes that stronger tool-calling models can be more vulnerable because they follow malicious tool instructions more effectively. MSB adds Net Resilient Performance (NRP) alongside ASR and Performance Under Attack to expose the security–utility trade-off.

##### Author-stated / evidence-based limitations

MSB is broad but still a controlled benchmark with a finite taxonomy and curated tools. Production MCP deployments can introduce server-specific permissions, authentication, transport and supply-chain conditions outside the 12 evaluated attack families.
